# Master Exam One-Liners & Previous Year Questions (PYQs)

## 1. Breeding, Reproduction & Genetics (PYQs)
* **First Scientific Research in AI:** Performed on dogs in **1780** by an Italian scientist.
* **Frozen Semen Storage:** Stored in **Liquid Nitrogen at -196 °C**.
* **Good Quality Semen Production Age:** Produced at the age of **22-24 months**.
* **Ejaculate Volume:** A full mature ram ejaculates a semen volume of about **1 ml**.
* **Ovulation & Mating Timing:** Ovulation is the shedding of ovum from the Graafian follicle. In cows, a single ovum can undergo fertilization only for **5-10 hours**, making precise timing crucial.
* **Optimum Insemination Time:** The optimum time to inseminate a cow during estrus is at the **end of estrus**.
* **Site of Semen Deposition:** The best site for semen deposition during artificial insemination in cattle is the **mid-cervix**.
* **Rebreeding Window:** Cows can be rebred in **9-12 weeks** after parturition.
* **Gestation Periods:**
  * Average gestation period of buffalo: **316 ± 5 days** **[IBPS-AFO 2020]**
* **Inter-calving & Inter-kidding Periods:**
  * Surti breed inter-calving period: **400-500 days** **[RRB SO 2018]**
  * Barbari goat inter-kidding period (kidded in January, successfully mated in April): **8 months**.
* **Age at First Calving:**
  * Murrah buffalo age at first calving: **42-45 months** **[IBPS-AFO 2018]**
* **Estrus Cycle & Phases:**
  * Implementation of the embryo takes place during **Metestrum** **[RRB SO 2018]**
* **Corpus Luteum Function:** A pregnant cow does not come into heat because **Corpus Luteum is present**.
* **Breeding & Mating Systems:**
  * Act of mating in sheep is called **Tupping** **[IBPS-AFO 2020]**
  * Mating of a wider degree of relation (not closer) resulting in uniformity is known as **Line breeding** **[IBPS-AFO 2020]**
  * Two well-developed breeds crossed alternatively is known as **Criss-cross breeding** **[IBPS-AFO 2021]**
* **Artificial Insemination Prerequisite:** **Semen sex sorting** is not a strict prerequisite for standard artificial insemination **[UP College Entrance 2023]**.

## 3. Dairy Science, Milk Composition & Processing Standards (PYQs)

### Milk Composition & Emulsion Properties
* **Type of Emulsion in Milk:** **Oil in water** **[IBPS-AFO 2020]**
* **Water Percentage in Cow Milk:** Approximately **87%**
* **SNF Standards (FSSAI):** Cow milk SNF is **8.5** **[IBPS-AFO 2021]**
* **Highest SNF Content:** **Sheep milk** **[IBPS-AFO 2018]**
* **Fat Content Extremes:**
  * Highest fat in exotic breeds: **Jersey** (produces >5%)
  * Second highest fat: **Buffalo**
  * Highest protein: **Sheep**
* **Colostrum Facts:**
  * Protein content: **4-5 times** normal milk (Normal milk: 3.5-4% protein; Cow colostrum: **21%**; Buffalo colostrum: **23%**)
  * First secretion of mammary gland following parturition **[RRB SO 2021]**
  * Feeding rate: **1/10th** of body weight for cow calves (within 15 minutes); **1/15th** of body weight for buffalo calves.
* **Milk Colors & Pigments:**
  * Yellow color of milk is due to **Carotenoid / Carotene** **[IBPS-AFO 2020, RRB SO 2019]**
  * Buffalo milk is whiter than cow milk because **Carotene is converted into colorless Vitamin A** **[IBPS-AFO 2021]**
* **A1 vs. A2 Milk & Amino Acid Linkage:**
  * Desi cow milk = **A2 milk** (healthier choice); Exotic cow milk = **A1 milk**.
  * Amino acid present in A1 milk at the **67th position** of the molecular chain but absent in A2 milk at the same position: **Histidine** **[RRB SO 2021]**

### Processing, Testing & Standards
* **Homogenization:** A process in which **large fat globules are broken into smaller particles** **[IBPS-AFO 2021]**
* **Pasteurization Purpose:** To destroy most pathogenic microorganisms (disease-causing bacteria).
* **Test for Incomplete Pasteurization:** **Alkaline Phosphatase test**
* **Water Content Testing:** Determined using a **Lactometer** (Dairy Farming).
* **Butter Specifications:**
  * Fat content: Generally about **80%**
  * Maximum moisture content legal standard: Must not exceed **20%**
* **Cheese & Dairy Products:**
  * Cheddar and cottage are types of **Cheese**.
  * Cheese contains about **40.3%** water.
* **Bacterial Fermentation:**
  * Milk carbohydrate fermentation: **Streptococcus lactis**
  * Milk conversion into curd: **Lactobacillus thermophilus**
  * Yoghurt production: *Lactobacillus bulgaricus* and *Streptococcus thermophilus*
* **Chemical Tests:** **R.M. Test (Reichert-Meissl test)** is used for **Ghee** **[IBPS-AFO 2021]**
* **Conversion Factor for Milk:** **6.38**

## 4. Feeds, Fodder, Nutrition & Ration Calculation (PYQs)

* **Ration Definition:** The amount of feed offered to an individual for a specific period.
* **Dry Matter Requirement:** **2-2.5 kg / 100 kg body weight** for Zebu cattle.
* **Calf Daily Weight Gain:** Well-fed crossbreed calves gain **400 grams a day** (2.5 to 3 kg per week) **[IBPS-AFO 2018]**
* **Concentrate Allowance for Pregnancy:** Exotic, crossbreed cow & buffalo in the **5th month of pregnancy** should receive **1.5 kg** concentrate (in addition to maintenance) **[IBPS-AFO 2022]**
* **Creep Feeding:** A place/ration used for feeding lamb, calves, and piglets that is **inaccessible to adults** **[IBPS-AFO 2020]**
* **Flushing:** Feeding extra concentrates to pregnant sheep; **increases lambing rate by 10-20%**.
* **Hay Making:** Fodder should be rich in **Proteins**. The best hay is formed from **Lucerne**.
* **Biological Value:** Percentage utilization of protein within the animal body.
* **Mineral & Feed Sources:**
  * Molasses is an excellent source of minerals.
  * Feeding cotton seed to buffaloes has **no effect on fat content** in milk.
  * Feeding green fodder to cows **decreases fat globule size**.
* **Swine Diet Restrictions & Creep Feed:**
  * Creep ration for pigs must contain **20% Crude Protein**.
  * Crude fiber in pig diets must **not exceed 10%**.

## 5. Pathology, Diagnostic Signs & Vaccines (PYQs)

### Infectious & Viral Diseases
* **Marek's Disease:** Found in **poultry**.
* **Bluetongue Disease:** Non-contagious, insect-borne viral disease of ruminants (mainly sheep, less frequently cattle, goats, buffalo) **[RRB SO 2019]**
* **Foot and Mouth Disease (FMD):** 
  * Highly infectious viral disease attacking **cloven-footed animals**.
  * Symptoms: Drooling of saliva, ulcers in gums and lips; suckling calves usually die **[UPCATET 2017, 19, 21]**.
  * FMD vaccine interval for sheep: **6 months** **[IBPS-AFO 2020]**
* **Japanese Encephalitis Amplifier Host:** **Pig** **[IBPS-AFO 2021]**
* **Diamond Skin Disease:** Related to **pigs** **[BHU 2017]**
* **Surra Disease Incubation Period:** **5-30 days** **[RRB SO 2019]**
* **Tuberculosis:** Caused by *Mycobacterium bovis*. Major symptoms include dry cough followed by wet cough.
* **Mastitis:** Inflammation of the mammary gland.
* **Black Quarter Pathognomonic Symptom:** **Crepitating sound** on pressing the affected part of the muscle **[MP PAT 2021]**
* **Avian Adenovirus Infection:** Characterized by straw-colored transudate in the pericardial sac with a pale, friable liver in 3- to 5-week-old broiler flocks **[RSMSSB AS 2021]**
* **Pink Eye in Bovines:** Caused by *Moraxella bovis* **[TNPSC VAS 2020]**

### Nutritional Deficiencies & Metabolic Disorders
* **Milk Fever (Parturient Paresis):** 
  * Caused by **Calcium deficiency** (abnormally low levels of calcium in blood of parturient cattle) **[IBPS-AFO 2016, RPSC]**
  * **No vaccine prepared yet** for milk fever **[RRB SO 2019]**
* **Rickets & Osteomalacia:** Caused by **Ca / P deficiency**.
* **Anemia:** Caused by **Fe deficiency**.
* **Pica:** Caused by **P deficiency**.
* **Wilson's Disease:** Caused by **Cu deficiency**.
* **Grass Tetany:** Caused by **Mg deficiency / K toxicity**.
* **Cannibalism in Poultry:** Caused by **NaCl deficiency**.
* **Crazy Chick Disease:** Caused by **Vitamin E deficiency**.
* **Curled Toe Paralysis in Chicken:** Caused by **Vitamin B2 deficiency** **[IBPS-AFO 2022]**
* **Dental Caries in Calves:** Caused by **Fluorine deficiency**.
* **Sodium Deficiency in Cows:** Causes **dehydration and fluid reduction** **[IBPS-AFO 2020]**
* **Excess Magnesium / Grass Feeding:** Excess feeding of grass causes **Magnesium deficiency**.
* **Tympanitis (Bloat):** Disorder caused by excessive feeding of lush green fodders in cattle **[AAO 2019]**

### Parasites, Zoonosis & Prevention Protocols
* **Zoonosis:** Transmission of disease from **animal to human** **[IBPS-AFO 2021]**
* **Ascaris Prevention in Calves (up to 6 weeks):** **Piperazine salts** **[RRB SO 2019]**
* **Coccidiosis in Swine:** Intracellular parasite resulting in **bloody diarrhea** (not a bacterial disease) **[IBPS-AFO 2020, RRB SO 2019]**
* **Brucellosis Prevention in Goats:** Isolation, vaccination, blood tests, and culling of affected animals **[IBPS-AFO 2022]**
* **Milk Ring Test (MRT / ABRT):** Dairy herds screened every **6 months** using bulk tank milk samples for Brucella antibodies. Positive reactors must be tested individually and slaughtered.
* **Vaccines & Institutes:**
  * Ranikhet disease vaccine and Goat Pox vaccine developed by **IVRI (Indian Veterinary Research Institute)** **[IBPS-AFO 2018, 2019]**
  * Rinderpest vaccine formulated by **IVRI** **[IBPS-AFO 2018]**
  * Ranikhet disease vaccine name: **Lasota** **[IBPS-AFO 2020]**

## 6. Farm Machinery, Bullock Draft Power, Housing & Economics (PYQs)

* **Animal Power & Draft Ratings:**
  * Area of land cultivated by animals: **0.33 Ha**.
  * Power rating of a full-grown bullock (pure Indian draught breed): **0.70 HP** (average is **0.5 HP**).
* **Tractor Performance:** A **35 HP tractor** can plough about **2.5 ha** of land in an eight-hour shift and consume about **5 liters of diesel/hour**.
* **Shoeing of Bullocks:** Should be done **once a month** for road work, and **once in two months** for field work.
* **Housing Dimensions & Space Requirements:**
  * Hen floor space requirement (NABARD norms): **1 sq. ft.** **[IBPS-AFO 2017, 2018]**
  * Pregnant cow floor space: **100-120 sq. ft.** **[IBPS-AFO 2018]**
  * Commercial layer farming housing system: **Battery cage** system (dimensions **14×16 inches**, height **17 inches**) **[IBPS-AFO 2021]**
  * Deep litter system: Litter material accumulated with poultry manure until it reaches a depth of **8-12 inches** **[RRB SO 2021]**
  * Stanchion barn: A place where feeding and milking sheds are put up together **[TNPSC Agriculture Officer 2021]**
* **Brooding & Rearing Gear:**
  * Application of heat to chicks during early life: **Brooding** **[RRB SO 2021]**
  * Apparatus used for keeping chickens warm: **Hover**.
* **Economics & Insurance:**
  * Livestock insurance premium is **5% of animal cost** **[TNPSC VAS 2020]**
  * Union Budget 2021-2022: Enhancement of agricultural credit focusing on animal husbandry, dairy, and fisheries to **16.5 Lakh Crore** in 2022 **[RRB SO 2021]**
  * KCC Interest Subvention: 2% per annum short-term loans up to **2 Lakh** for fisheries and animal husbandry **[NABARD 2021]**

## 7. Poultry & Avian Science (PYQs)

* **Breed Origins & Characteristics:**
  * Kadaknath: Originated from Dhar district of M.P.; produces **black-colored meat** **[RRB SO 2021]**
  * Aseel: Breed of hen famous for **fighting sports**; has the **highest weight at the age of 5 weeks** **[IBPS-AFO 2015, 2018]**
  * Plymouth Rock: Excellent for **broiler purpose**.
  * White Leghorn: Excellent **layer**. (If a farmer has 1 lakh White Leghorn birds, annual egg production is **220 lakh**).
  * Backyard poultry main breeds: Kalinga Brown, Mumbai Desi, Rhode Island Red, and CARI Nirbheek.
  * Rhode Island Red: Useful for both **meat and egg production**.
* **Broiler Management & Production:**
  * Ideal broiler batch system in India: **5-6 batches** at any time.
  * Number of poultry batches reared in a year: **7** **[IBPS-AFO 2018]**
  * Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR) in broiler chicken: **1.5 to 1.9** **[IBPS-AFO 2020]** (Highest FCR overall found in poultry and swine).
  * Broiler chicken meat is known as **white meat** (sold as white meat and chicken breast) **[NABARD 2021]**
  * Dressing percentage in broiler poultry: **70-75%** **[TNPSC 2023]**
* **Poultry Terminology & Management:**
  * Castrated male chicken: **Capon**.
  * Feed requirement of a hen in total economic life: **55 kg**.
  * Recommended time to purchase layer chicks: **February to April**.
  * Commercial egg production method: **Commercial poultry** **[NABARD 2021]**
