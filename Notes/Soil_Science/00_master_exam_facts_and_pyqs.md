## 1. Fundamentals, National Institutes & Global Benchmarks (PYQs & Facts)

### Fundamental Physical Constants & Environmental Benchmarks
* **Solar Constant**: The rate of solar radiation reaching the outer limit of the earth's atmosphere is **2 cal/cm²/min**.
* **Surface Tension of Water**: **72.7 dyne/cm²** at **25°C**.
* **Water Molecule Geometry**: Bond angle of **104.5°** with a hexagonal lattice arrangement in solid state.
* **Colloidal Size Threshold**: Soil colloids possess particle diameters of **< 1 µm (< 0.001 mm)**.
* **Soil vs. Air Temperature**: Mean annual soil temperature is approximately **1°C higher** than the mean annual air temperature.
* **Standard Temperature Measurement Depths**: International Meteorological Organization (founded **1873**, restructured **1950**) recommends standard depths of **10 cm, 20 cm, 50 cm, and 100 cm**.
* **Redox Potential ($E_h$) Thresholds**:
  * Well-drained / well-aerated soil: **+400 to +700 mV**
  * Waterlogged / submerged soil: **-250 to -300 mV**
* **Soil Loss Permissible Limit**: **12 t/ha/year** in India.
* **Volumetric Conversion Benchmark**: $1\text{ ha-cm} = \mathbf{100,000\text{ Liters}} = 100\text{ m}^3$.
* **C:N Ratio Benchmarks**:
  * Tropical soils: **10:1**
  * Immobilization threshold: **> 30:1** (leads to net $N$ immobilization)
  * Soil Microbes: Lowest C:N ratio among organic sources **[RRB SO-2020]**

### Diagnostic Deficiency Thresholds & Symptom Identification
* **Iron (Fe)**: Deficiency occurs at **< 50 ppm** in plant tissue; causes interveinal chlorosis on young leaves where main veins remain green **[IBPS-AFO 2019]**, **[IBPS-AFO 2020]**. Common in high pH calcareous soils.
* **Manganese (Mn)**: Deficiency occurs at **< 25 ppm**.
* **Boron (B)**: Highly deficient at **< 0.33 ppm** soil concentration. Acts as a "fuel pump" aiding sugar translocation from mature leaves to roots/meristems.
  * *Boron Deficiency Indicator Plants*: Sunflower, Lucerne, Coconut, Guava **[IBPS-AFO 2021]**.
  * *Boron Sensitivity*: Cabbage is sensitive to Boron toxicity. Causes citrus dieback and apple cracking **[IBPS-AFO 2016]**, **[IBPS-AFO 2017]**.
* **Zinc (Zn)**: Most widespread micronutrient deficiency in Indian soils. Causes "Little Leaf" in Mango and Cashew, and "Mottle Leaf" in Citrus **[IBPS-AFO 2016]**. Paddy is highly responsive to Zn application **[IBPS-AFO 2015]**.
* **Nitrogen (N)**: Most deficient macronutrient in Indian soils. Deficiency symptoms appear first on **older lower leaves** and advance upward **[IBPS-AFO 2016]**, **[IBPS-AFO 2017]**.
* **Phosphorus (P)**: Essential for root development and energy transfer **[IBPS-AFO 2017]**.
  * *P-Requirement Order*: Potato > Wheat > Maize > Rice, Millets > Legumes > Cotton, Jute.
* **Potassium (K)**: Deficiency causes leaf scorching/marginal necrosis.
* **Sodium (Na) Indicator Plant**: Sugar Beet.

### Organic Carbon & Organic Matter Conversions
* **Van Bemmelen Factor**: Organic Matter ($\text{OM}$) = Organic Carbon ($\text{OC}$) $\times$ **1.724** **[IBPS-AFO 2016]**.
* **Organic Carbon Utility**: Serves as an indirect measure of available Nitrogen in soil **[AP VAA-2019]**, **[IFFCO AGT 2019]**, **[HAU Entrance]**.
* **Humus Composition**: Lignoprotein complex containing **40–45% Lignin** and **30–33% Protein**. Dark color of humified organic matter is due to **Humin**.

## 2. Rocks, Minerals & Weathering Resistance Series (PYQs & Facts)

### Elemental Distribution in Earth's Crust
* **Primary Crustal Minerals**: **Feldspars (48%) > Quartz (36%) > Micas (10%)**.
* **Structural Unit**: Silica Tetrahedron ($\text{SiO}_4^{4-}$) is the fundamental building block of all silicate minerals.

Silica Tetrahedron [SiO4]4- ---> Single/Double Chains/Sheets ---> Silicate Mineral Framework

Minerals exhibit differential stability to weathering based on Goldich’s Dissolution Series.

| Resistance Level | Mineral Name | Chemical Nature / Characteristics | Exam Tags |

| **Most Resistant** | **Quartz** ($\text{SiO}_2$) | Framework silicate, non-cleavable | Standard Benchmark |
| **High Resistance** | **Muscovite (White Mica)** | Potash mica $\text{KAl}_2(\text{AlSi}_3\text{O}_{10})(\text{OH})_2$ | Comparative Fact |

* **Mica Weathering Comparison**: White Mica (Muscovite) is significantly **more resistant** to weathering than Black Mica (Biotite).

### Igneous, Sedimentary & Metamorphic Igneous Minerals
* **Igneous Origin**: **Granite** is an intrusive igneous rock (NOT sedimentary) **[IBPS-AFO 2021]**.
* **Aluminum Mineral Source**: **Bauxite** ($\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3\cdot n\text{H}_2\text{O}$) is the primary ore of aluminum **[Maharashtra Forest Mains 2019]**.
* **Boron Mineral Source**: **Tourmaline** is a complex borosilicate mineral **[RRB SO-2019]**.
* **Sand Fraction Primary Mineral**: **Quartz** dominates the coarse sand fraction of soils.
* **Adobe Soil Definition**: Soils formed by broken rock material transported jointly by wind and water agents.

* **Illuviation (B Horizon)**: Zone of accumulation of materials (clays, sesquioxides, humus) leached from upper horizons **[IBPS-AFO 2020]**.

## 7. Soil Chemistry, Colloids, Ion Exchange & Organic Matter Fractions (PYQs & Facts)

### Fundamental Rocks, Minerals, and Soil Genesis Facts
* **Primary to Secondary Transformation:** Transportation and cementation of primary rocks yields **Sedimentary (Secondary) Rocks** **[JRF 2018]**.
* **Metamorphic Rocks:** Formed via the thermal and pressure alteration of pre-existing primary and secondary rocks **[JRF 2018]**.
* **Igneous Rocks:** **Granite** is a classic intrusive igneous rock **[UPCATET PG, BHU, AFO 2021]**. Granite is **NOT** a sedimentary rock **[AFO 2021]**.
* **Boron Mineral Sources:** **Tourmaline** and **Borax** are the primary mineral sources of Boron in soils **[RRB SO 2019]**.
* **Magnesium & Potassium Minerals:** **Dolomite** ($CaMg(CO_3)_2$) and **Muscovite** ($KAl_3Si_3O_{10}(OH)_2$) **[MCAER PGCET 2018]**.
* **Soil Fertility Definition:** The inherent capacity of a soil to supply essential plant nutrients in adequate amounts and suitable proportions for plant growth **[UPCATET PG, BHU, AU PG, IFFCO AGT]**.

---

### Cation Exchange Capacity (CEC) & pH Interactions
* **Sandy Soils & CEC:** Sandy soils exhibit extremely low Cation Exchange Capacity (**CEC**); to minimize leaching losses, soluble fertilizers must be applied in **split doses** **[MCAER PGCET]**.
* **Nutrient Availability at pH < 6.0:** At **pH < 6.0**, the solubility and availability of **Al, Fe, Mn, Zn, Cu, and Co** are significantly elevated, often approaching phytotoxic levels if pH drops too low **[MCAER PGCET]**.
* **Black Soil Color:** The characteristic dark color of black cotton soils (Vertisols) is attributed to **Titaniferous Magnetite** associated with humified organic complexes.

### Soil Organic Matter (SOM) & Biological Transformations
* **Van Bemmelen Factor:** Soil Organic Matter (**SOM**) content is calculated from Organic Carbon (**OC**) using the standard conversion factor:
  $$\text{Organic Matter (\%)} = \text{Organic Carbon (\%)} \times \mathbf{1.724} \quad \mathbf{[AFO\ 2016]}$$
* **SOM Range in Indian Soils:** Typically ranges between **1\% and 5\%** **[BHU 2014]**.
* **Ammonification:** Microbially mediated enzymatic breakdown of organic nitrogenous compounds (amino acids, amides) into free ammonia ($\mathbf{NH_3/NH_4^+}$) under aerobic conditions by heterotrophic microorganisms **[BHU 2018]**.
* **Nitrification:** Biological two-step oxidation of ammonium to nitrate under aerobic conditions by autotrophic bacteria:
  1. $2NH_4^+ + 3O_2 \xrightarrow{\textit{Nitrosomonas}} 2NO_2^- + 4H^+ + 2H_2O + \text{Energy}$
  2. $2NO_2^- + O_2 \xrightarrow{\textit{Nitrobacter}} 2NO_3^- + \text{Energy}$ **[AFO 2015]**
* **Denitrification:** Anaerobic reduction of soil nitrate ($\mathbf{NO_3^-}$) into gaseous nitrogen ($\mathbf{N_2}$) or nitrous oxide ($\mathbf{N_2O}$) by facultative heterotrophic bacteria (*Pseudomonas*, *Bacillus*). Accelerated by **waterlogging** and high soil **pH** **[AFO 2015]**.

### Pedological Processes & Horizonation
* **Eluviation (E or A2 Horizon):** The "washing out" or removal of soil constituents (clay, Fe, Al, organic matter) in suspension or solution from upper layers to lower layers by percolating water **[RRB SO 2019]**.
* **Illuviation (B Horizon):** The "washing in" or deposition of eluviated soil materials into lower soil horizons **[AFO 2020]**. Zone of illuviation is the **B Horizon** **[AFO 2020]**.
* **Colluvial Transport:** Soil particles and parent rock debris deposited at hill slopes by gravity are termed **Colluvial / Colluvium** **[RRB SO 2020]**.
* **Organic (O) Horizon:** Visible predominantly in **virgin forest soils** and entirely absent in cultivated **arable soils** due to tillage mixing **[BHU Exam]**.
