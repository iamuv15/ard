## 2. Bovine Science (Cattle)

The genus *Bos* is broadly divided into:
*   *Bos indicus*: Tropical, humped cattle.
*   *Bos taurus*: Temperate, hump-less cattle.

### Functional Classification of Cattle Breeds
1.  **Milch / Milk Breeds**: High milk yielders, average >1,600 kg per lactation (range: 1,500–2,500 kg). Male animals are slow or poor work animals.
2.  **Dual-Purpose Breeds**: Average milk yield (500 kg to 1,500 kg); male animals are very useful for draft work.
3.  **Draught Breeds**: Cows are poor milk yielders (<500 kg per lactation); male animals excel at work. 
    *   *Draft Performance Benchmark*: A pair of bullocks can haul **1,000 kg net** with an iron-tired cart on a good road at a walking speed of **5 to 7 km/h**, covering **30–40 km per day**.

## 3. Bubaline Science (Buffaloes)

### Classification of Buffalo Types
1.  **Swamp Buffalo (*Bubalus carabanesis*)**: 
    *   Exotic buffalo; body color copper and dark brown.
    *   Very low milk yield; used mainly as draft animals (e.g., Karabaow, Sui-new, Sinhala).
    *   Found in southwest regions; powerful load-carriers.
    *   **Chromosome Number**: $2n = 48$.
2.  **River Buffalo (*Bubalus bubalis*)**: 
    *   Indian buffalo, milk buffalo, or water buffalo.
    *   Body color mostly black (Exceptions: Bhadawari and Surti are copper-colored).
    *   Found in plains and low hill areas of India.
    *   **Chromosome Number**: $2n = 50$.
    *   Primary source of milk supply in India (Buffaloes contribute maximum to overall Indian milk, whereas cows lead globally).
    *   *Flax*: The shiny flesh of a dead buffalo calf.

## 5. Caprine Science (Goats)

*   **Scientific Name**: *Capra hircus* (Family: Bovidae).
*   **Economic Profile**: Known as the **"Poor man's cow"** and **"Enemy of vegetation"**.
*   **Special Fibers/Leather**: 
    *   *Pashmina*: Finest natural fiber obtained from Changthangi and Chegu (Kashmiri) goats.
    *   *Mohair*: White wool-like hair from the Angora breed (average fleece yield: 1.2 kg).
    *   *Morocco Fine Leather*: Prepared from goat skin.

### Functional Utility Categorization of Goats
*   **Milch Breeds**: Toggenburg, Saanen, Alpine, Anglo-Nubian, Boer, Beetal, Jamunapari.
*   **Dual-Purpose Breeds (Milk + Meat)**: Beetal, Jamunapari.
*   **Meat & Skin Breeds**: Black Bengal, Kanni Adu, Barbari.
*   **Hair & Meat Breeds**: Marwari, Malabari (Tellicherry), Sirohi, Surti.
*   **Fur Breeds**: Pashmina, Kashmiri.
*   **Exotic Dairy Breeds**: Toggenburg, Saanen, Alpine, Anglo-Nubian, Boer, Angora.

## 6. Ovine Science (Sheep)

*   **Scientific Profile**: Family Bovidae, Genus *Ovis*, Species *aries*. Reared primarily for meat and wool.
*   **Water Requirement**: Adult sheep require **2–4 liters** daily.
*   **Wool Potential**: Exotic breeds: **3.5–5 kg**; Indian breeds: **1–2 kg**.

### Wool Quality Classifications (Indian Breeds)
*   **Superior Carpet Wool**: Magra, Chokla, Nali, Bikaneri.
*   **Coarse Wool**: Marwari, Coimbatore, Bellari.
*   **Good Quality Wool**: Gurez, Bharwal, Gaddi, Rampur Bushair.
*   **Apparel Wool Breeds**: Hissardale, Nilgiri, Kashmir Merino, Keezhakaraisal.
*   **Hairy/Mutton Breeds**: Trichy Black, Ramnad White, Malpura, Vembur, Madras, Nellore, Mandya.
*   **Exotic Fine Wool Breeds**: Merino, Rambouillet, Polwarth, Corriedale.
*   **Pelt Breed**: Karakul.

### Sheep Management Practices
*   **Shearing**: Complete removal of wool using machine or hand shears. Treat cuts immediately with *tincture of iodine* or *gentian violet*.
*   **Dagging / Crutching**: Cutting away dirty, wet wool from around the tail and anus to prevent blowfly strike (maggot infestation). Symptoms: restless, stamping feet, wriggling tail.

## 8. Swine Husbandry (Pig Farming)

*   **Taxonomy**: *Sus scrofa vittatus*, *Sus scrofa indicus* (Group stock: Drove).
*   **Anatomical Feature**: **Monogastric** (single-compartment stomach).
*   **Production Economics**:
    *   Highest feed conversion efficiency among meat animals (except broilers). **FCR = 1:2.5 – 3**.
    *   Highly prolific: Farrows **10–12 piglets** per litter; 2 litters per year.
    *   High carcass yield (dressing percentage): **65–80%** (Desi: 66%, Exotic: 68%).
    *   Pork is rich in thiamin, niacin, and riboflavin.
    *   Marketable weight attained within **6–8 months**.

### Swine Management Parameters
*   **Back Fat**: Fat deposited along the back. Excessive back fat is undesirable (desired range: 4–5 cm).
*   **Barrel**: Body part between fore and rear legs.
*   **Slop-Feeding**: System of mixing dry feed with fluid (water or skim milk) to form a slurry.
*   **Flushing**: Feeding a good grower ration to sows/gilts **7–10 days before breeding** to increase ovulation rates.
*   **Boar Selection & Management**:
    *   Final selection at **7 months** (min weight: **90 kg**).
    *   Put to regular use at **10–12 months** (min weight: **100 kg**).
    *   Boar:Sow ratio = **1:10 – 25**. Services: **2–4 per week**.
    *   Test periodically for *Brucellosis* and *Leptospirosis*.
*   **Culling Sows**: Cull old sows after **10–12 farrowings**.

### Indigenous & Regional Pig Breeds in India
*   **Exotic Breeds**: Hampshire, Large White Yorkshire, Duroc, Landrace, Tamworth.
*   **Indigenous Breeds**: Ghungroo, Niang Megha, Ankamali, Agonda Goan, Tenyi Vo, Nicobari, Doom, Zovawk.

*   **Ghungroo**: North Bengal. Black color, typical **"Bulldog" face**, litter size 6–12. Highly docile.
*   **Tenyi Vo (Native Naga Pig)**: Nagaland (Chakesang, Mao, Tuensang, Angami). Potbellied, sagging back, pendulous belly touching ground in females, straight tail ending with white marking reaching hock joint.
*   **Doom**: Assam. Black, straight topline, long bristles extending to thoraco-lumbar area.

## 9. Camel Science (Camelidae)

*   **Origin**: North America.
*   **Zoological Classification**: Phylum: Chordata $\rightarrow$ Class: Mammalia $\rightarrow$ Order: Artiodactyla $\rightarrow$ Sub-order: Tylopoda (**pad-footed**) $\rightarrow$ Family: Camelidae $\rightarrow$ Genus: *Camelus*.
*   **Species**: 
    1.  *Camelus bactrianus* (Two-humped Bactrian camel)
    2.  *Camelus dromedarius* (Single-humped Arabian camel / Dromedary)
*   **Digestive Status**: **Pseudo-ruminant** (ruminates, but possesses distinct polygastric/morphological dissimilarities).
*   **Dental Formula (Full Mouth)**: 
    *   Upper: $\frac{6\text{ Incisors} + 2\text{ Canines} + 2\text{ Wolf teeth} + 10\text{ Premolars} + 12\text{ Molars}}{2} = 16$ (per side, total 34)
    *   Lower: $\frac{6\text{ Incisors} + 2\text{ Canines} + 0\text{ Wolf teeth} + 16\text{ Premolars} + 18\text{ Molars}}{2} = 18$ (total teeth = 34)

### Indian Camels: Regional Types & Utility
*   **Desert Varieties**: Arid sandy tracts of Bikaner & Jaisalmer (Rajasthan).
    *   *Riding*: Jaisalmeri, Bikaneri.
    *   *Baggage*: Afghani, Sindhi.
*   **Plain / Riverine Varieties**: Western UP and Punjab. Carry heavy loads; slow speed (~3 km/h).
    *   *Baggage*: Mewati.
*   **Hill Camels**: North Punjab hills. Shorter, compact, muscular body, short neck, wide chest, round and hard feet. Used for loading, riding, and ploughing.

### Reproductive Behaviour & Parameters (Camels)
*   **Puberty & Breeding Age**: Puberty at ~3 years; bred at **4 years** (early breeding stunts growth).
*   **Breeding Season**: Strictly winter months (**November to March**). Productive breeding life up to 20–30 years.
*   **Oestrous Cycle**: **22 to 24 days** (Rajasthan camels); oestrus lasts **3 to 5 days**.
*   **Gestation Period**: **365 to 400 days** (Average: **390 days**; first pregnancy prolonged by 10–20 days).
*   **Parity**: Single calf, breeds once every two years. Suckling for 1 year.
*   **Abortion**: Common, usually caused by *Trypanosomiasis* or feed scarcity.

### Major Camel Diseases
| Disease Name | Local Name | Causal Organism | Clinical Symptoms | Control / Treatment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Anthrax** | Sool | *Bacillus anthracis* | Sudden death without apparent cause, high temperature, blood discharge from natural openings. | Carcass destruction, strict sanitation. |
| **Trypanosomiasis** | Surti, Tibarsa, Mbori | *Trypanosoma evansi*, *T. congolense*, *T. debab* | Dropping neck, loss of appetite, edema, transmitted by biting flies during rainy season. | Anti-trypanosomal drugs. |
| **Rabies** | - | Lyssavirus (Rhabdoviridae) | Paralysis of lower jaws, salivation, tongue hangs down, vicious biting behavior. | Anti-Rabies Vaccine. |
| **Jhooling** | - | Suspected fungus or *Streptococcus* bacteria | Painful local fibrous tumors on neck, hindquarters, and abdomen. | Red iodide of mercury; redress with potassium permanganate after 3–4 days. |

## 10. Oryctolagiculture (Rabbit Production)

*   **Taxonomy**: Class: Mammalia $\rightarrow$ Order: Lagomorpha $\rightarrow$ Family: Leporidae $\rightarrow$ Genus: *Oryctolagus* $\rightarrow$ Species: *Oryctolagus cuniculus* (True European rabbit).
*   **Global Production**: Europe accounts for 85% of world output (China is second).
*   **Rabbit vs. Hare**: 
    *   *Rabbit*: Short ears/legs, nests in underground burrows, young born blind and naked (*altricial*).
    *   *Hare*: Long ears/legs, does not burrow, nests above ground in vegetation, young born furred and open-eyed (*precocial*).

### Reproduction & Management (Rabbits)
*   **Ovulation Type**: Induced ovulator (does *not* have a regular oestrous cycle; ovulation occurs **only after mating**). Acceptance = heat; refusal = diestrus.

*   **Kindling**: Occurs mostly at night; lasts 15–30 minutes. Kits weigh **30–80 g**. Hair appears on day 4; eyes open on **days 10–14**.
*   **Weaning**: 4–6 weeks. Breeding frequency: 4–6 times/year.

## High-Yield Exam Traps Summary (ICAR / AFO / NABARD)

> [!WARNING]
> *   **Chromosome Trait Trap**: River Buffalo ($2n=50$) vs. Swamp Buffalo ($2n=48$). Do not confuse these with Cattle ($2n=60$) or Goats ($2n=60$).
> *   **Fat Content Superlative**: **Bhadawari** buffalo milk has the highest fat content (9–12.5%), whereas **Jersey** cattle milk has the highest fat among exotic dairy cows.
> *   **Goat Titles**: Saanen = *"Queen of milk of the goat world"*; Anglo-Nubian = *"Jersey cow of the goat world"*; Jamunapari = Longest lactation length.
> *   **Pig FCR & Dressing**: Swine possess the highest FCR (1:2.5–3) and dressing percentage (65–80%) among meat-producing livestock.
> *   **Camel Pseudo-Ruminant Status**: Camels are pseudo-ruminants with 34 teeth and a gestation period averaging 390 days (strictly winter breeders).
> *   **Rabbit Ovulation**: Rabbits are **induced ovulators**—they do not exhibit a standard estrus cycle; ovulation requires mating stimulation.
